The administrative panel is named **Dive | Hub** and controls the products, content, students, and access for the member area.
Use the supplied Dive | Hub horizontal logo in expanded brand placements and the blue flag icon in compact placements and the favicon.
Do not display BYB or The BYB branding anywhere in the interface.
Keep brand images compressed and appropriately sized to preserve loading speed.
